Networked Non-Places is an atlas of international airports reframed as relational geography. Instead of mapping airports on the Earth's surface, it redistributes them by connectivity — drawing densely linked pairs together while peripheral airports drift outward, exposing how visually identical non‑places occupy radically different roles in planetary mobility.
